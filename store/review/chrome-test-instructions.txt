No account. Needs an iPhone with the free Nodkey app from the App Store, 1.4 or later.
1. Toolbar icon > "Get a code". In Nodkey on the iPhone: Agents > Add agent, type the code. Confirm the same check number on both screens.
2. On a sign-in page of a site saved in Nodkey, click the Nodkey button in the field. Approve on the iPhone.
3. New in {version}: in a new password field of a sign-up, click the Nodkey button, then "Make a strong password". Approve. Both password fields are filled.
